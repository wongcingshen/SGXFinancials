Saved standards

The Claude connector uses your standards too. The Claude buttons on a filing send its company’s standard along by themselves; for chats you start yourself, copy them all and paste them into a Claude project’s instructions (or the chat).

Add to Claude

Use SGX Financials from a Claude chat. Photo and scanned filings are read by Claude itself.
  1. 1Click Add to Claude. Claude opens the Add custom connector dialog with the name and link already filled in.
  2. 2Click Continue. In a chat, turn it on from the + menu → Connectors, then ask e.g. “Get last Friday’s SGX results for Bank of Baroda into Excel.”

Text filings are read by this server’s AI. When the statements are pictures (scans, photos), the connector hands Claude the cleaned-up pages and Claude reads the figures itself, then this server runs the tie-out checks and builds the Excel file.

How hard AI reads

Balanced suits most filings. Thorough helps with banks, REITs and dense layouts, at higher cost.

Cost of goods sold (COGS)

When a filing prints no cost of sales, AI names the company’s industry and marks which expense lines are COGS by that industry’s rule (materials always go in); the program adds them up, and the workbook’s COGS Basis sheet shows every line, in or out, and why. Change a rule, add an industry, or give a company its own.
